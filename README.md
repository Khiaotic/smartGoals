<h1 align="center">SMART Goal Generator </h1>

## ✨Overview (description)✨
<h3 align="center">Smart Goal Generator is the easiest way to organize, plan, and reach your short term and long term goals. SMART is an abbreviation of the five rules: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, and Time-Bound. The service and free of charge and does not require any subscription or sign-up. This generator takes into account a person's biogenic, sociogenic, and idiogenic characteristics to expand on how one's personality is involved in creating and achieving goals.</h3>
