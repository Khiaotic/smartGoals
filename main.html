ABOUT

Smart Goal Generator is the easiest way to organize, plan, and reach your short term and long term goals. SMART is an abbreviation of the five rules: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, and Time-Bound. How To

Smart goals come in many forms. Business, Health and Fitness, Personal Finance, Hobbies, and more.

How to generate a SMART goal: There are 5 parts of SMART

  1. Specific

    2. State the exact thing you want to achieve. Who, what, when, where, and why. Consider wording it in a positive light. Ex:"Lose (x amount) of weight" vs "Learn to enjoy alternative ways to be more active in order to maintain my physical health"

  2. Measurable

    3. Understand how to track progress (numbers, data, experiences, etc). Ex: "Follow a training program three days a week" or "Make conscious choices to be active 3 days a week and keep a journal record"

  3. Achievable

    4. Define and acknowledge the action-responsibility clearly. Use action-oriented words. Ex: "Develop the habit of walking after at least 1 meal a day and track in a journal" or "Plan to work out for 20mins every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday"

  4. Relevant

    5. Create goals that matter to your life or values. What makes this goal worthwhile? What keeps you inspired and motivated?Ex: "I value my health and wellness and this active goal will help me sustain that" or "Being a personal trainer has been a passion of mine, which motivates me to start this health journey"

  5. Time-Bound

    6. Provide realistic time constraints (1 week, 3 months, a semester, specific date, etc). If a deadline is long, consider breaking down milestones at specific intervals. Ex: "I will maintain a 3 day workout week for 1 month and weigh myself at the beginning and end of the month" or "My next doctor's appointment is Aug.25th, I want the scale to have gone down"

Answering the 15 personality questions will result in how your personality may play an affect in the results of your goals and projects. Answer the questions in regard to each individual smart goal. Results may differ with each smart goal. The personalty questions are inspired by Brian Little's personality "Big Five" OCEAN framework: Openness (how a person responds to new ideas), Conscientiousness (connected to impulse control), Extraversion (your brain's active reward system response), Agreeableness (baseline empathy and and cooperative luring), and Neuroticism (inherited sensitivity to negative emotions or potential threats). These results are interpretative (I am not a psychologist or scientist). Use #khiaoticsmartgoal on socials to post your goal results and how your personality may have affected the results.
